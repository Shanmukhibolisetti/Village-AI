The Problem

The skills gap is growing.
Rural children are falling behind.

City schools have coding labs, AI tools, and industry connections. Most rural schools have none of this. Village AI exists to close that gap — permanently.

🏙️

City vs Village Gap

Children in urban India are learning Python, AI, and data science from Grade 3. Rural children are still learning basic MS Paint. The gap widens every year.

💼

Jobs Require New Skills

The fastest-growing jobs in India — in tech, data, AI, and digital services — require skills that rural students are simply never taught. Not because they cannot learn. Because no one taught them.

🧑‍🏫

No Trained Teachers

Rural schools want to upgrade. But qualified computer and AI teachers are concentrated in cities. Schools cannot hire what is not available.

📱

Awareness is Missing

Parents and principals in rural areas have not been shown what AI is, why it matters, and how it will affect their children's futures. The conversation has not reached them yet.

How It Works

Simple for schools. Powerful for students.

Village AI handles everything — teacher, curriculum, materials, and support. The school simply provides the classroom and the students.

01

We partner with your school

Village AI meets with the principal, understands the school's setup, and agrees on the programme structure. No financial outlay from the school.

02

We deploy a trained IFSAI teacher

One certified Village AI teacher is placed at each school branch. Your existing computer staff works alongside as support — and we pay their salary for that role.

03

IFSAI replaces the computer period

No new period is added. IFSAI slots into the existing computer class. Zero timetable disruption. Zero hiring by the school.

04

Students learn, build, and present

Every term ends with a showcase event where students present their projects to parents — building confidence alongside technical skills.
